Qivvo Privacy Policy

Effective 7 September 2026

Qivvo is a party game app for one phone. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how you can delete it. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
An anonymous account IDThe first time you open the appTo remember which questions you have already played so they are not repeated, and to store your own questions
Your email addressOnly if you sign inTo let you sign in on another phone and to attach your Party Pass purchase to you rather than to a device
Your display name and avatar colourWhen you set themShown on your own screen. Never shown to other users.
Questions you writeWhen you write themPrivate to your account. Questions you choose to suggest are reviewed by a person and may be added to the shared library without your name.
Player names you type into a partyDuring a partyKept on the phone and in your party record for the length of the party, deleted automatically within 24 hours
Purchase statusIf you buy a passProcessed by Apple or Google and RevenueCat. We store only whether your account holds a pass. We never see card details.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics SDKs that profile you.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with anyone for their own purposes.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. The app does not ask for these permissions.
  • No content is generated by artificial intelligence. Every question was written by a person.

Who processes data on our behalf

  • Supabase (database and sign-in). Data is stored in their cloud infrastructure.
  • RevenueCat (purchase records), together with Apple App Store or Google Play, which handle payment.

Each of these providers is bound by its own privacy terms and processes data only to provide the service to us.

Retention and deletion

Your account data is kept until you delete your account. You can delete it at any time inside the app: Profile → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, your questions, your played-question history and your pass record from our systems. Party records are deleted automatically within 24 hours of a party ending.

If you no longer have the app, you can request deletion by emailing hello@qivvo.app from the address you signed in with. We complete requests within 30 days. See also our account deletion page.

A purchase made through Apple or Google remains restorable through them even after you delete your Qivvo account; they hold that record, not us.

Children

Qivvo is rated 17+ and is not directed at children. The Kids pack exists so families can play together on an adult's phone. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data at any time using the app or by emailing us. If you are in the EU, UK or a jurisdiction with similar law, you also have the right to object to processing and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you the next time you open it.
